# Metatranscriptomics: CST association with riboflavin genes

## DGE_riboflavin_genes.qmd

Tables from the paper [Insight into the ecology of vaginal bacteria through integrative analyses of metagenomic and metatranscriptomic data](https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13059-022-02635-9) 
are retrieved from [the corresponding github repository](https://github.com/ravel-lab/MTMG_longitudinal).

Results are filtered for riboflavin genes (on KEGG identifiers) and plotted in a jitterplot (for relative abundances of the transcripts) and volcano plot (for the dream DGE results of the riboflavin genes).
